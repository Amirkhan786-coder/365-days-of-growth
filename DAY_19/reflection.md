## My Biggest Learning

The biggest learning from Day 19 was understanding how
Regular Expressions and Collections can work together.

Regex can extract useful information from raw text, while
Collections can count, group, and organize that information.

For example:

Raw Text
    ↓
Regex
    ↓
Extract Data
    ↓
Counter / defaultdict
    ↓
Analyze Data
    ↓
Useful Information
